Nine Lives

An AI fires a car's 10 cold-gas thrusters so it always lands on its wheels. Drive, jump, flip, and watch it save you nine times.

Watch AI training

The car carries ten air nozzles and a tank. In limited air the tank runs down; in unlimited air it never does (M switches). The AI fires the nozzles in the air to turn the car so the wheels touch first. Each crash costs a life; you have nine.

W S drive and brake, A D steer, Space hops, Shift boosts. Q / E barrel-roll and Z / B flip when the planner says a stunt is ready. Esc pauses.
